# Digital price tags (electronic shelf labels): what a furniture floor costs
Generated: 2026-10-25 ~06:00 PDT · Agent: research-fork · Status: partial (no vendor quotes; published ranges only)

For the proposed fourth pricing level ("Sync", working name): electronic shelf labels on every piece that show the approved tag price, later the ML pricing suggestions once approved. Sized for a store like the interviewed one: about 300 pieces on the floor (the demo store has 300 items, 704 units).

## Summary
- Standard e-paper labels (2.9 to 4.2 inch) cost about $5 to $20 each; size drives price, and a 4.2-inch color label can pass $15. [F-esl-01]
- Large labels sized for furniture placards cost far more: the one concrete 7.5-inch price found is $68.99 per unit at a 10-piece minimum from an unverified wholesale supplier. Opticon makes a 7.5-inch label with no published price. Treat $40 to $70 as the planning range until a vendor quotes. [F-esl-02]
- A base station (gateway) runs about $300 to $1,500 and covers 1,000 to 3,000 labels, so one or two cover a showroom. Management software is about $500 up front plus a monthly fee. [F-esl-01, 03]
- A small pilot of 100 to 200 labels with basic software is about $2,000 to $6,000 all in. [F-esl-03]
- In furniture, labels have only been rolled out at scale by Nebraska Furniture Mart (about 30,000 tags, 2014) and piloted in a German store's accessories area (2025). [F-tools-18]

## Is $5,000 setup reasonable?
Yes, as a service fee kept separate from hardware. $5,000 covers install, mapping every SKU to a label, connecting the label software to the approved tag price, and training. It sits at the top of the published "small pilot" range for the work alone, and it is about 1 to 2 months of a full-floor store's subscription, which is a normal ratio for onboarding fees. Recommendations:
- Keep hardware at cost and separate, and say the floor size drives it: about $4,000 to $8,000 for standard labels on 300 pieces, more for placard-size labels.
- Offer a smaller entry: $2,500 setup for up to 150 big-ticket pieces. Most of the margin is in sofas, sectionals and dining sets, and the owner can expand later.
- Monthly: Forecast's $450 plus a tag-sync fee. Custom is fine for "talk to us"; if a number is needed, $150 a month on top of Forecast covers the label software subscription and support.
- Nothing about this level is live: it depends on ML pricing and a label vendor. Every line on the page should say so.
