# Voice of customer: video and audio (YouTube, TikTok, podcasts)
Generated: 2026-10-24 · Agent: research-voc-video · Status: partial (YouTube complete; TikTok and Instagram blocked; podcast platforms yielded show notes only)

## Summary
- The "let me check with my manager" walk is described from both sides: a 35-year salesperson calls it theater where "the better number was there all along" [Q-video-04], a furniture-sales trainer scripts it as a closing move [Q-video-23], and a consumer coach tells shoppers that a manager appearing means "keep pushing" [Q-video-01].
- The floor already exists and is hidden on the tag: a former store employee says the lowest price was written backwards on the back of the price tag [Q-video-02]; a mattress-factory owner describes a chain that tags at 3x cost, floors at 2x, and bonuses reps for anything above the floor [Q-video-25].
- Free delivery is the universal give: consumers are coached to ask for it [Q-video-03, Q-video-09, Q-video-10], a 35-year salesperson warns it reappears at the register [Q-video-07], and a Texas owner refuses to charge for it against conference advice [Q-video-30].
- Salespeople are paid on delivery, not on the handshake, and the write-up is reworked afterward (revisions, price matches, cancellations, "house the sale"), which is where commission disputes come from [Q-video-16 to Q-video-19, Q-video-22].
- Owners say they do not track ticket-building or discount behavior: "I don't look at any of that stuff" [Q-video-29]; a new owner names inventory management as the hardest part [Q-video-35].
- Price-protected brands (UPP/MAP) are the one place a rep has no discretion; over-discounting them "can get you fired" [Q-video-14]; showrooming is a daily owner complaint [Q-video-28].
