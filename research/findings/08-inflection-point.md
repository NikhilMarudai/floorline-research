## Summary
- "1 in 5 small businesses have been bought out" cannot be sourced. The defensible "1 in 5" is: 20% of small-business owners have been approached by a buyer (BizBuySell 2025 Insight Report). The stronger decline fact is: 92% of small-business exits are closures, only 5% are sales (McKinsey, Feb 2026, on 2022 Census data) [F-inflect-01, 02].
- Furniture stores shrank 6.6% in ten years: 23,513 US establishments (2013) to 21,964 (2023), while the Top 100 retailers' share of furniture-store sales rose from 62% (2011) to 80% (2016) and ~85% (2019–2020) [F-inflect-07, 08].
- Roll-ups are measurable in adjacent SMB categories: PE-affiliated dentists doubled 6.6% to 12.8% (2015–2021); ~25% of general vet practices are corporate-owned (2024) vs ~10% (2018); ~24 of the top 100 CPA firms took PE money since 2021; ~800 HVAC/plumbing/electrical firms bought by PE since 2022 [F-inflect-06].
- AI adoption trend, three independent series: generative-AI use by small businesses 23% (2023) → 40% (2024) → 58% (2025) (US Chamber/Teneo); regular AI use by US SMBs 48% (Jul 2024) → 77% (Jan 2026) (Intuit); all US firms using AI in any function 17–20% (Dec 2025–May 2026), retail ~14% (Census BTOS) [F-inflect-10, 11, 14].
- Adopters report better outcomes, correlationally: 43% of AI-using SMBs say revenue rose vs 2% fell (Intuit 2026); 82% of AI-using small businesses grew headcount (Chamber 2025); 33% of business buyers see AI-adopting businesses as more valuable (BizBuySell 2025) [F-inflect-12, 14, 15].
- Era markers: e-commerce 7.4% of US retail in Q4 2015 → 16.7% in Q4 2025; Square reader shipped 2010, Square Stand and Shopify POS 2013; ChatGPT 30 Nov 2022; small-business gen-AI use crossed 50% in 2025 [F-inflect-18, 19].

### F-inflect-03 — 6 million US SMBs face ownership exits by 2035; only ~1 million are viable for sale

## Gaps and unverified
- Furniture Today Top 100 share of furniture-store sales for 2021–2025: not found (newest share figure is 84% for 2020). The 2025 and 2026 Top 100 articles are paywalled beyond the teaser.
- Private-equity or roll-up penetration in furniture retail specifically: nothing found; furniture consolidation evidence is chain share (Top 100), store-count decline and anecdotal legacy-store closures.
- Current (2024–2025) e-commerce share of furniture sales and Wayfair share: only 2019–2020 analyst estimates; aggregator figures conflict.
- Car-wash roll-up penetration: not searched within the time box.
- Exit Planning Institute figures (42% written plan, 58% boomer five-year exit) are quoted from a WashU Olin white paper and a CEPA study guide, not the EPI PDF. Treat as medium confidence until the PDF is opened.
- Goldman Sachs 10KSB (68%/76%) and BizBuySell 2025 annual (9,586 deals; 20% approached; 33% buyers value AI) come from named secondary write-ups; primary pages returned 403 in this session.
- Census CBP 2013–2014 use 2012 NAICS and 2019–2023 use 2017 NAICS; the 442110 definition is unchanged, but CBP methodology changed in 2017 (noise infusion), so treat the ten-year change as approximate (±1 point).
- The Census BTOS series has a wording break in Nov 2025; do not plot 2024 and 2026 BTOS points on one line without a note.
- Bipartisan Policy Center small-business AI survey: not found in time.
- All adoption-outcome figures (Intuit, Chamber, Deloitte) are self-reported and correlational; no study found that links AI adoption to small-business survival or sale price.
