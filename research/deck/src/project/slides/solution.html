Solution

Two numbers on every item.

Tag price

Moves with age, sales and trends. Set once by the owner’s rules.

Minimum

Checked on the salesperson’s phone. Nobody sells below it.

No phone call to the owner. Sales set in one place.

Every item gets a price that can change: when it’s been sitting too long, when it’s selling fast, when something’s trending. And a minimum, the lowest the store will take. The salesperson types the offer into their phone, ticks free delivery if they want it thrown in, and gets yes, or no with two counteroffers. Sales get set in one place, with a printed list of which tags changed.
