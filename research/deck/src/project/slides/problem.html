Pricing means weighing cost, freight, trends, color, brand. Most owners don’t have time, so they multiply by about two and never touch it again. Salespeople are paid on the sale, so a discount barely costs them. On sale days someone retags every piece by hand. And every discount turns into a phone call: customers wait, and some of them leave.
